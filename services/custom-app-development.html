FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to build a custom app?

Simple internal tools can be built in 2-4 weeks. More complex applications with multiple features and integrations typically take 6-12 weeks. We provide a detailed timeline after the discovery phase.

What technologies do you use?

We use modern web technologies including React, Next.js, Node.js, Python, and PostgreSQL. The specific stack is chosen based on your requirements, scalability needs, and integration requirements.

Do I own the code?

Yes, 100%. You receive full ownership of the source code, database, and all application assets. There are no ongoing licensing fees or vendor lock-in of any kind.

Can the app integrate with our existing tools?

Absolutely. We build applications that integrate with your existing systems — CRM, accounting software, communication tools, cloud storage, and any platform with an API.

What about hosting and maintenance?

We handle deployment to your preferred hosting provider and offer ongoing maintenance plans. We can also set up self-hosted solutions if you prefer to manage infrastructure in-house.
