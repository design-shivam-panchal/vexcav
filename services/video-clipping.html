FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How many clips can you create from one video?

It depends on the content length and density. A typical 30-60 minute video yields 8-15 quality clips. Longer content like 2-hour podcasts can produce 20+ clips.

Do you add captions to the clips?

Yes, every clip includes burned-in captions with custom styling that matches your brand. We use dynamic caption animations that boost engagement and watch time.

What video formats do you work with?

We work with all standard video formats — MP4, MOV, MKV, and more. We can pull content from YouTube links, Google Drive, Dropbox, or any file sharing service.

Can I choose which moments to clip?

Absolutely. You can provide timestamps for specific moments, or let our editors identify the best clips. Most clients use a combination of both approaches.

Do you offer recurring clipping packages?

Yes, we offer weekly and monthly clipping packages for creators who publish regularly. These come at a discounted rate with a dedicated editor assigned to your content.
