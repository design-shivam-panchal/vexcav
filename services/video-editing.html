FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What formats do you deliver the final videos in?

We deliver in all standard formats including MP4, MOV, and platform-specific exports for Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, and LinkedIn. Custom formats are available on request.

How fast can you turn around a video edit?

Standard turnaround is 48-72 hours for most projects. Rush delivery within 24 hours is available for an additional fee. Bulk projects follow a custom timeline agreed upon upfront.

Do I need to provide a script or storyboard?

Not necessarily. While scripts and storyboards help, we can work with raw footage and a brief. We will structure the narrative and pacing based on your goals and target audience.

Can you add subtitles, motion graphics, and music?

Absolutely. We include subtitles/captions, custom motion graphics, royalty-free music, sound effects, and brand elements as part of our standard editing workflow.

How do revisions work?

Every project includes unlimited reasonable revisions. After the first draft, you share feedback, and we iterate until you are fully satisfied with the final output.
