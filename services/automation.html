FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What tools do you use for automation?

We work with Zapier, Make (Integromat), n8n, custom API integrations, Google Apps Script, and more. The choice depends on your existing stack and requirements.

How long does it take to set up an automation?

Simple automations can be built in 2-3 days. Complex multi-step workflows typically take 1-2 weeks. We provide a detailed timeline after the initial workflow audit.

Will the automations break if I change tools?

We build automations with modularity in mind, making it easier to swap out tools when needed. We also provide documentation so adjustments can be made efficiently.

Do I need technical knowledge to manage the automations?

No. We design automations to be low-maintenance and provide training and documentation. For complex systems, we offer ongoing management as part of our support plans.

How much can I expect to save with automation?

Most businesses save 15-30+ hours per week depending on the processes automated. The ROI typically becomes positive within the first month of deployment.
