FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to build a website?

A standard business website (5-8 pages) takes 2-4 weeks. Landing pages can be delivered within 1 week. Complex websites with custom functionality may take 4-8 weeks depending on scope.

Do you use WordPress or custom code?

We work with both. For most projects, we build custom-coded websites for maximum performance and flexibility. WordPress is used when clients need easy self-management of content.

Will my website be mobile-friendly?

Absolutely. Every website we build is responsive and mobile-first. We test on multiple devices and screen sizes to ensure a flawless experience everywhere.

Do you handle hosting and domain setup?

Yes. We can set up hosting, configure your domain, install SSL certificates, and handle all the technical aspects of getting your website live and running smoothly.

What about ongoing maintenance?

We offer monthly maintenance plans that cover updates, security monitoring, performance optimization, and content changes. Post-launch support is included for 30 days at no extra cost.
