FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How many posts per month do you publish?

Our standard plans include 20-30 posts per month across platforms. Custom plans with higher volume are available based on your needs and platform strategy.

Which platforms do you manage?

We manage Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Pinterest, and Google Business Profile. We recommend platforms based on where your target audience is most active.

Do you handle community management and replies?

Basic community management (responding to comments and DMs) is available as an add-on. We can set up response templates and escalation protocols for your brand.

Can I approve content before it goes live?

Absolutely. We share the monthly content calendar and all creatives for your review and approval before anything is scheduled or published.

Do you run paid social campaigns too?

Our social media management focuses on organic content. Paid social advertising can be added as a separate service with dedicated campaign management and reporting.
