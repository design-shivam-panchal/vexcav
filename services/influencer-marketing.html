FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum budget for an influencer campaign?

We work with budgets starting from $2,000 for micro-influencer campaigns. The optimal budget depends on your goals, target audience, and the number of creators needed.

How do you find the right influencers for my brand?

We use a combination of influencer discovery tools, manual research, and our existing creator network. Every influencer is vetted for audience authenticity, engagement rates, and brand fit.

Do you handle contracts and payments?

Yes, we manage the full negotiation process including deliverables, usage rights, timelines, and payment terms. All agreements are formalized before any work begins.

How do you measure campaign success?

We track reach, impressions, engagement rate, clicks, conversions, and cost-per-result. A detailed campaign report is provided with insights and recommendations for future campaigns.

Can you run campaigns on specific platforms?

Yes, we run campaigns across Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Twitter, and LinkedIn. We recommend platforms based on where your target audience is most active and engaged.
