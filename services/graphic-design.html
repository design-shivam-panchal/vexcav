FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What file formats do you deliver?

We deliver designs in PNG, JPG, SVG, PDF, and source files (Figma, PSD, AI). You will receive web-optimized and print-ready versions as needed.

Can you match our existing brand guidelines?

Absolutely. We work within your existing brand system — colors, fonts, logo usage, and tone. If you do not have formal guidelines, we can help establish them.

How many designs can you deliver per month?

This depends on the plan you choose. Our monthly retainer packages range from 20 to 100+ designs per month, with custom plans available for larger needs.

Do you design for print as well?

Yes, we handle both digital and print design. Business cards, brochures, packaging, event banners, and more — all delivered in print-ready specifications.

How do revisions work?

Each design includes multiple revision rounds. We work closely with your feedback to ensure the final output matches your vision perfectly.
